## Description

Stronger AI is the workout tracker with an actual coach inside.

It builds your routines, plans your week, tracks every PR, and remembers your training — your injuries, goals, and weak points — so every session is tailored to you, not generic.

Ask it anything: "What should I train today?" · "Am I overtraining legs?" · "Build me a 4-day push/pull split."

AI COACH
Chat with a coach that knows your full history and current numbers. Real, context-aware guidance — never copy-paste tips.

BUILD ROUTINES INSTANTLY
Tell the AI what you want and it builds (and edits) a complete routine in seconds. Or create your own reusable templates.

PLAN YOUR WEEK
Schedule sessions and recurring splits onto your calendar. Always know exactly what's next.

TRACK EVERY PR & 1-REP MAX
Automatic PRs and estimated 1RM across every lift, with volume trends and per-muscle balance. See exactly when and how you hit each milestone.

A COACH THAT REMEMBERS YOU
The AI takes notes across sessions — your injuries, weak points, preferences, and goals. Every conversation builds on the last.

LOG FAST, REST SMART
A clean, gym-floor-fast interface for sets, reps, and weight, with a built-in rest timer — and a live workout on your Lock Screen & Dynamic Island so you never need to open the app mid-set.

EXERCISE LIBRARY
Hundreds of exercises with muscle-group targeting. Add your own custom movements.

Train smarter. Get Stronger.
